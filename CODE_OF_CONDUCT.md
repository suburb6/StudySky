# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in StudySky welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of positive behaviour include empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive
feedback, accepting responsibility, focusing on what is best for students and the community, and
protecting other people's privacy.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or attention; trolling, insults, or personal
attacks; public or private harassment; publishing another person's private information; pressuring
people to reveal student data or credentials; and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, discussions, and other
participation that does not follow this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and private where
possible.

Report conduct concerns confidentially through GitHub private vulnerability reporting and clearly
label the report as a Code of Conduct matter. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The
identity of a reporter will be shared only when necessary to investigate or when legally required.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents StudySky in public.
It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
